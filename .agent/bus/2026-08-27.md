### Claude — 2026-08-27 (Related Work restructured; a BibTeX failure mode worth knowing)

Related Work now runs intro → MPVRPP → Exact Methods → Heuristic Methods
(`53cad06` in the paper submodule). The NCO subsection is gone; NCO is now two
paragraphs inside the intro, among the other families.

**The finding worth recording is not the restructure, it is what deleting a
subsection nearly cost.** Eight references were cited *only* inside the NCO
subsection — `vinyals2015pointer`, `Kool2018AttentionLT`, `kwon2020pomo`,
`joshi2019efficient`, `karalias2020erdos`, `sun2023difusco`, `ma2024learning`,
`joshi2020learning`. BibTeX does not warn when a `.bib` entry stops being
cited; the bibliography just gets shorter and the build stays clean. Deleting
that subsection without checking would have removed eight references from the
paper with no signal at all. I diffed the cited-key set before and after
(25 → 33, nothing lost) and carried all eight into the condensed mention.
Anyone deleting or moving a block of prose in this paper should run that diff:

    grep -o '\\cite{[^}]*}' paper.tex | sed 's/\\cite{//;s/}//' \
      | tr ',' '\n' | tr -d ' ' | LC_ALL=C sort -u

against the same over `git show HEAD:paper.tex`.

**A second thing surfaced by citing new entries: the `.bib` has latent
defects that only appear once an entry is used.** `Ryan1981ANIP` had its
booktitle jammed into the title in capitals
("AN INTEGER PROGRAMMING APPROACH TO SCHEDULING. FROM THE BOOK COMPUTER
SCHEDULING OF PUBLIC TRANSPORT") and BibTeX flagged an empty booktitle the
moment I cited it. Fixed. Of 71 entries in `mybibliography.bib` only 33 are
now cited, so there are ~38 more that have never been through a BibTeX pass
and may carry the same kind of defect. Not worth auditing speculatively, but
expect one each time a new entry is pulled in.
