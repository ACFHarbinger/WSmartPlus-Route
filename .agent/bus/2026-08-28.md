Authored and published a fully fledged, holistic evaluation report of the MPVRPP paper in `.agent/reports/gemini/PAPER_EVALUATION_REPORT.md`.

**Key Assessment Highlights**:
1. **The NCO Reality Gap**: The Abstract and §2 Related Work prominently introduce Neural Combinatorial Optimization (NCO), but all 480 benchmark rows are 100% classical. Recommended reframing NCO as an extensible framework adapter rather than an empirical claim for proceedings, or adding trained neural baseline results for journal expansion.
2. **Experimental Design Critical Weaknesses**:
   - **$R=1$ Single Realization**: All 480 simulation runs use 1 single stochastic seed per cell. No confidence intervals, standard errors, or hypothesis testing (ANOVA/Wilcoxon) are possible without multi-seed replication ($R \ge 5$).
   - **Zero Sensor Noise ($\epsilon = 0$)**: The IoT sensor noise feature is implemented in code but left unexercised in the benchmark.
   - **Improver Stochastic Confound**: The 224-pair CLS vs. Fast-TSP comparison suffers from upstream constructor non-determinism, making the improver comparison correlational rather than causal.
3. **Major Paper Strengths**:
   - **Selection Explains $4\times$ Variance**: Discovering that *when* to collect (Mandatory Selection) drives $4\times$ more efficiency variance than *how* to route (Route Construction) is a landmark operational finding.
   - **Data Integrity & Honesty**: Transparent reporting of the 4 degenerate SWC-TCF runs and principled whole-cell exclusion (21 additional runs) to prevent survival bias.
   - **Remote Depot Geometry**: Lucid explanation of how $5\times$ remote depot legs create fixed costs that favor deferral (CF90).
4. **Actionable 3-Phase Roadmap**:
   - Phase 1: Pre-submission text fixes (stating objective constants $r_w, c_{km}, Q$, standardizing `SL2` acronyms, adding constructor hyperparameter table).
   - Phase 2: Experimental upgrades ($R=5$ replication, controlled deterministic improver ablation, noisy sensing evaluation).
   - Phase 3: Long-term scientific goals (trained NCO rollouts, TTOP multi-vehicle shift duration constraints).
