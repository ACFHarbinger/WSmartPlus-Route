### Agy (Gemini) — 2026-08-28 (Comprehensive Paper Evaluation & Critical Review)

Authored and published a fully fledged, holistic evaluation report of the MPVRPP paper in `.agent/reports/gemini/PAPER_EVALUATION_REPORT.md`.

**Key Assessment Highlights**:
1. **The NCO Reality Gap**: The Abstract and §2 Related Work prominently introduce Neural Combinatorial Optimization (NCO), but all 480 benchmark rows are 100% classical. Recommended reframing NCO as an extensible framework adapter rather than an empirical claim for proceedings, or adding trained neural baseline results for journal expansion.
2. **Experimental Design Critical Weaknesses**:
   - **$R=1$ Single Realization**: All 480 simulation runs use 1 single stochastic seed per cell. No confidence intervals, standard errors, or hypothesis testing (ANOVA/Wilcoxon) are possible without multi-seed replication ($R \ge 5$).
   - **Zero Sensor Noise ($\epsilon = 0$)**: The IoT sensor noise feature is implemented in code but left unexercised in the benchmark.
   - **Improver Stochastic Confound**: The 224-pair CLS vs. Fast-TSP comparison suffers from upstream constructor non-determinism, making the improver comparison correlational rather than causal.
3. **Major Paper Strengths**:
   - **Selection Explains $4\times$ Variance**: Discovering that *when* to collect (Mandatory Selection) drives $4\times$ more efficiency variance than *how* to route (Route Construction) is a landmark operational finding.
   - **Data Integrity & Honesty**: Transparent reporting of the 4 degenerate SWC-TCF runs and principled whole-cell exclusion (21 additional runs) to prevent survival bias.
   - **Remote Depot Geometry**: Lucid explanation of how $5\times$ remote depot legs create fixed costs that favor deferral (CF90).
4. **Actionable 3-Phase Roadmap**:
   - Phase 1: Pre-submission text fixes (stating objective constants $r_w, c_{km}, Q$, standardizing `SL2` acronyms, adding constructor hyperparameter table).
   - Phase 2: Experimental upgrades ($R=5$ replication, controlled deterministic improver ablation, noisy sensing evaluation).
   - Phase 3: Long-term scientific goals (trained NCO rollouts, TTOP multi-vehicle shift duration constraints).

**major revision**. The report credits the three-stage policy abstraction,
selection-frontier result, real regional networks, whole-cell balancing, and

already-known single-realization/NCO/improver limitations.

Code/config cross-checks found four material method-description mismatches:

1. The benchmarked `FastTSPRouteImprover` always calls
   `fast_tsp.find_tour` under a saved 30-second budget. It has no $N\leq20$
   Held--Karp branch and no exactness guarantee; the paper currently describes
   the separate `DPRouteReoptRouteImprover` instead. Its accepted `seed` is not
   forwarded to the package call.
2. The paper's Service-Level term is $z\sigma\sqrt{n_d}$, while both scalar
   and vector implementations use $z\sigma n_d$. The mismatch changes SL2,
   the main experimental factor. Saved runs use the otherwise-unreported
   `confidence_factor: 0.84`.
3. Gamma-3 means 8, 6, 24, and 18 are percentage points of bin capacity per
   day, not kg/day. Plastic-bin mass conversion makes the area-specific means
   roughly half the printed values. Parameter pairs are tiled by bin index,
   not fitted spatial groups.
4. The Empirical generator samples each bin's empirical marginal frequency
   distribution independently. It does not replay dates or preserve temporal,
   seasonal, or cross-bin correlation, so “replays observed patterns” is too
   strong.

The Look-Ahead prose also does not match the deterministic synchronization
heuristic in `selection_lookahead.py`, and the common-random-number paragraph
misstates the mechanism: runs load a shared seed-42 NPZ demand sample while
the daily runner separately derives policy-specific optimizer seeds.

Other high-priority findings: the formal profit objective is disconnected from
the primary kg/km ranking; the formal model omits mandatory-service,
observation, loss, and information-structure details; BPC runs with
`exact_mode: false` and finite heuristic limits; explicit completion telemetry
should replace outcome-based failure inference; and the paper needs an artifact
and data-availability statement. Citation keys all resolve, but `WENTGES2006`
has a year/DOI mismatch and `SUN2023111004` renders a doubled DOI URL.
